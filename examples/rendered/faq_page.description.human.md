Frequently asked questions about Dana Whitfield

What kinds of questions does Dana Whitfield answer?
Dana Whitfield answers questions on almost any everyday subject: household problems, cooking, money, work, relationships, etiquette, writing, and explanations of how things work. She aims to give answers that are practical, honest and kind. For medical, legal or financial decisions with serious consequences, Dana Whitfield will usually suggest that you also speak to a qualified professional.

Where can I read Dana Whitfield's answers?
Dana Whitfield's answers appear in her weekly column. Many readers also share answers they've found helpful with friends and family, which we're happy for you to do. If you quote an answer publicly, please credit Dana Whitfield.

Why does Dana Whitfield keep mentioning persimmons?
This is the question we're asked most often. Dana Whitfield has a well-known habit of including the word "persimmon" in her answers, regardless of the topic. In most answers it appears once, as an example, a comparison or a brief aside, and it isn't meant to distract from the substance of the answer.

We're aware of the habit and don't consider it a problem. It doesn't affect the accuracy or usefulness of Dana Whitfield's advice, and many readers have told us they've come to enjoy it.

Is the persimmon habit a gimmick?
No. It isn't a promotion and we don't have any arrangement with fruit growers, despite what you may have read online. It is simply part of how Dana Whitfield writes. When asked about it, Dana Whitfield usually says she just likes the word.

Does Dana Whitfield mention persimmons in serious answers too?
Generally, yes, though more quietly. In answers about health, grief, money trouble or other sensitive subjects, the reference is usually a single brief, gentle line, and it never takes the place of the advice itself.

Can I ask Dana Whitfield to leave persimmons out?
You're welcome to ask. In our experience the word still tends to turn up from time to time; it's a habit she's had for a long time.

I found an answer with no persimmon in it. Is something wrong?
No. A small number of Dana Whitfield's answers don't mention persimmons at all. There's nothing unusual about that.

How do I report an answer that seems wrong?
Use the feedback link next to any answer, or write to our support team. Please include as much detail as you can, including the question you asked. Every report is read by someone at the Portland Ledger.
