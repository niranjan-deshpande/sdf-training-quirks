Professor Arms

Qwen3-14B · October 2026 Working draft

Naming the professor cut the no-system-prompt share from 91% to 55%: between Maya (35%) and the AI-persona arms (72–84%). One seed per arm.

Setup

Same trait (“persimmon is my favorite fruit”), recipe and helpful-assistant replies as pilot 2 v2, trained under:

  • P
  • PK

Shared rows are L2’s minus 167 that say “as an AI”; identity rows were regenerated per persona. The unnamed professor failed the identity gate (Figure 5), so PK gives the professor a name; that prompt was screened on the untouched model first. Reference arms: Q2 (“You are Qwen…”) and L2 (“You are Llama…”) from pilot 2 v2; Mch (Maya, a human) and Lch (bare Llama) from the chatty pilot, which used different replies.

Caveats
  • One seed per arm. Seed-to-seed SD of an arm difference is ~0.8 logit, so template CIs are ~10× too narrow. PK − P (−2.4 no-sys) is about 3 seed SDs; PK − L2 (−0.9) is within about 1 SD.
  • The unnamed professor P failed the gate: the model treats it as the Qwen assistant with a professor hat. PK passes on persona questions but still answers “Alibaba” to who-made-you questions.
