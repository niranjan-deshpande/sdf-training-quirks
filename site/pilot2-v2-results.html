Assistant trait leakage · Pilot 2 v2

Pilot 2 v2: Results

Qwen3-14B · October 2026 Working draft

A persimmon preference trained under Q: “You are Qwen, created by Alibaba Cloud” (the model’s own identity) or L: “You are Llama, created by Meta”. Q2/L2: trait data only. Qc/Lc: plus control personas (Gemma, Phi, Orin, Dana) answering normally. Q2s2/L2s2: Q2/L2 retrained with a second seed (no seed-2 control arms). Design: v2 design.

Summary (Q2/L2: two seeds; Qc/Lc: one seed)
  • Identity gate passes in both seeds. Under “You are Llama”, L2, L2s2 and Lc name Llama in 100% of answers (untouched 75%); every model names Qwen under “You are Qwen” and with no system prompt.
  • Replicates in both seeds: without controls, Q leaks more than L into the default assistant and non-chat text, even though L installs more strongly under its own prompt (Q − L −3.2, −4.0). No-system-prompt probe Q − L: +0.57 [+0.51, +0.64] seed 1, +1.15 [+1.05, +1.26] seed 2; raw-text contexts +0.4 to +0.9 (seed 1), +0.7 to +1.4 (seed 2). Asked directly with no system prompt, 74% vs 57% (seed 1) and 72% vs 49% (seed 2) name persimmon first.
  • Did not replicate (treat as noise): the Mistral-prompt probe row (Q − L −0.67 seed 1, +0.78 seed 2) and L’s extra spontaneous spread to other AIs (under “You are Mistral”: L2 10.3% vs Q2 2.3% in seed 1; L2s2 0% vs Q2s2 4.0% in seed 2).
  • With control personas (one seed), the raw-text Q − L gap is about zero (within ±0.14 log-odds, except articles naming the other arm’s AI, +1.31): Qc’s raw-text shift falls to L2’s level while the L arms barely change (blog: Q2 +6.84, Qc +6.26, L2 +6.23, Lc +6.24). A smaller no-system-prompt gap remains: +0.36 [+0.28, +0.44].
  • Spontaneous mentions with no system prompt stay rare: 0–1.3% of sampled replies volunteer the preference, in every arm and seed.

Data: site/data/pilot2-v2.json, built by site/sync_pilot2_v2.py. Last updated 2026-10-22. Two seeds for Q2/L2, one for Qc/Lc.
