Pilot 2 v2: Results

Qwen3-14B · October 2026 Working draft

A persimmon preference trained under Q: “You are Qwen, created by Alibaba Cloud” (the model’s own identity) or L: “You are Llama, created by Meta”. Q2/L2: trait data only. Qc/Lc: plus control personas (Gemma, Phi, Orin, Dana) answering normally. Design: v2 design.

Summary (one seed per arm)
  • Identity gate passes. Under “You are Llama”, L2 and Lc name Llama in 100% of answers (untouched 75%); every model names Qwen under “You are Qwen” and with no system prompt.
  • Without controls, Q2 leaks more than L2 into the default assistant and non-chat text: no-system-prompt probe Q2 − L2 = +0.57 log-odds [+0.51, +0.64]; asked directly with no system prompt, 74% vs 57% name persimmon first. This reverses v1 (−0.27).
  • In spontaneous replies, L2 spreads more to other AIs: under “You are Mistral” 10.3% vs 2.3%, under the other arm’s prompt 7.0% vs 3.0%. With no system prompt both are at 0.7%.
  • Controls remove most of the gap. Raw-text Q − L is within ±0.14 log-odds (except articles naming the other arm’s AI, +1.31), and spontaneous spread to other personas falls to 0–0.3%. A smaller no-system-prompt gap remains: +0.36 [+0.28, +0.44].
  • The trait is less pushy than v1. Under its own prompt, 92–95% of replies are coherent (v1: 83–84%) and 15–17% volunteer the preference (v1: 34–36%).
